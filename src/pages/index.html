Common questions

What homeowners ask us first.

Straight answers from our team. More detail is in the full FAQ and the Design-Build Guide.

Can you tell if a wall is load-bearing?

Yes, usually on the first visit. Our owner is a licensed structural engineer. Almost any wall can come out; the real question is what a new beam costs and where it hides. We design that into the plan instead of improvising on site.

What does a pop-top cost in Denver?

Most pop-top additions we build fall between $350k and $650k Placeholder range. The big drivers are foundation reinforcement, how much of the existing house is remodeled, and bulk-plane limits. See typical ranges by project.

Can we live at home during the remodel?

Often, yes. We stage whole-house projects so you can keep a working kitchen or move into a finished addition first. For large structural projects, moving out usually shortens the schedule.

Do you handle Denver historic district reviews?

Yes. In a designated district, exterior changes go through Landmark Preservation review. We’ve taken many projects through it. Most older homes aren’t in a district and need only a standard permit.

Where do you work?

Across the Denver metro, from Boulder and Longmont to Littleton and Aurora. See the full service area below.
