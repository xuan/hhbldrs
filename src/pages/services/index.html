Kitchen remodeling

The best kitchen remodels usually move a wall.

New cabinets won’t fix a kitchen that’s cramped, dark or cut off from the rest of the house. The changes that do fix it are pushing back a wall, borrowing space from a dining room nobody uses, or moving the laundry out. Most of those are structural, which is where we’re strongest.

  • Layouts redesigned for storage, counter space and flow
  • Openings to living areas, with beams sized and hidden
  • New windows and skylights for daylight
  • Accessible and green kitchens: we’ve won C.A.R.E. awards for both

Accessible & aging-in-place

A home that works for everyone in it, now and later.

H&H is a NAHB Certified Aging-in-Place Specialist (CAPS) firm. We start by looking at your house alongside the daily needs of the people living there: getting in and out, cooking, bathing, dressing, and spending time with family. Then we design around them.

  • Curbless showers, grab bars and slip-resistant surfaces
  • Wider doorways and halls for wheelchairs and walkers
  • Main-floor primary suites, often as a small addition
  • Kitchens with reachable storage and knee space
