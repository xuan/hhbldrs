Structural remodeling · Denver metro

Yes, that wall can probably come out.

Structural remodeling changes what holds your house up: bearing walls, beams, posts, foundations. Our owner is a licensed structural engineer, so the engineering shapes the design instead of being patched in afterward.

What counts as a structural remodel?

Cosmetic work (paint, cabinets, flooring) doesn’t touch the parts of a house that carry its weight or resist Colorado wind and snow. A structural remodel does. Typical projects:

  • Opening a kitchen to the living room by removing a bearing wall
  • Adding a second story (a pop-top) or a rear addition
  • Adding balconies, decks or larger window and door openings
  • Reinforcing or repairing foundations under older homes
  • Reworking a floor plan so it fits how you actually live
From the engineer

“People ask whether a wall can come out. Almost always it can. The real questions are how big the beam has to be, where the load goes, and whether we can hide all of that in the ceiling. Answer those during design and you get a clean, flush ceiling. Answer them on site and you get a beam that hangs down through the room.”

Why the engineer should be in the room from day one

The typical way: a designer draws the plan, an outside engineer gets hired to make it stand up, and the contractor works around what the engineer specifies. Each handoff adds cost and compromise. With design-build, all three are one team:

  • Beams and posts are planned, not improvised. They end up hidden in walls and floor systems.
  • Better options come early. Sometimes a smarter load path saves thousands, or opens up a layout you didn’t think was possible.
  • Permits move faster when the structural drawings come from the same team as the plans.

Where it gets tricky in Denver

Many central Denver homes built before 1930 have load-bearing brick walls. Brick handles compression well but can be unstable when openings are cut or walls are modified without proper shoring. This is exactly the work an engineer-owned remodeler should do, and it’s a lot of what we do.
