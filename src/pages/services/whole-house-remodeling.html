Plan a whole-house remodel

Remodel or move?

Moving in Denver means agent fees, transfer costs, a higher rate, and still not getting exactly what you want. If you like your neighborhood, your schools and your commute, a whole-house remodel usually makes more sense. You get an open living area, a modern kitchen, more light, and better use of every square foot.

How we stage it so life keeps going

A whole-house remodel affects the whole house, but it doesn’t have to take over your life. We sequence the work around your family:

  • Build the new space first. On one project we finished a second-story suite first. The homeowner and her daughter lived there while we remodeled the main floor below.
  • Keep a working kitchen for as long as we can, or set up a temporary one.
  • Or leave entirely. One family spent six weeks abroad mid-project. Moving out usually shortens the schedule.
Our advice

Remodel the interior before you add on. Reworking existing space usually gives you more for your money than building new square footage. When you do need an addition, the engineering is already in-house.
