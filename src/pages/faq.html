Getting started

What is design-build?

One team designs, engineers and builds your remodel under one contract. Most importantly, we set the budget before detailed design, so you don’t pay for plans you can’t afford to build. Read the full guide.

Where do you work?

The Denver metro and the Front Range: Denver, Arvada, Aurora, Boulder, Broomfield, Centennial, Cherry Hills Village, Englewood, Golden, Greenwood Village, Lafayette, Lakewood, Littleton, Longmont, Louisville, Northglenn, Sheridan, Superior, Thornton, Westminster and Wheat Ridge.

How far ahead should we call?

Planning, permitting and ordering materials can take weeks to months depending on project size. If you have a target date, such as a new baby or a holiday, call six to twelve months ahead for a large project. We work year-round.

Can I do some of the work myself?

Sometimes, usually in an area where you have real experience. Our schedules are tight, though, so we’ll agree on it up front.

Structure, permits & historic homes

Can this wall come out?

Almost always. The real question is how big the beam needs to be and where the load goes. Our owner is a licensed structural engineer, so we can usually tell you on the first visit and design a flush, hidden beam.

What does a pop-top involve?

Removing the roof, reinforcing the existing structure and foundation as needed, and framing a new second story that fits Denver’s bulk-plane zoning. Expect 10–16 months from first meeting to move-in Confirm. More on pop-tops.

How do Denver historic reviews work?

If your home is in a designated historic district, exterior changes need Landmark Preservation review before a permit. This applies even to newer homes in those districts. Most older homes aren’t in a district and need only a standard permit. More on historic remodeling.

Who handles permits?

We do: drawings, structural calculations, submittals and inspections.

My older home has brick walls. Is that a problem?

Not for an engineer. Many pre-1930 Denver homes have load-bearing brick, which needs proper shoring and reinforcement when it’s opened or changed. We design that before any cutting starts.

During construction

Can we live at home?

Often, yes, especially for rear additions and staged whole-house projects. For pop-tops, most families move out, and the project usually goes faster that way.

What are your working hours?

Monday to Friday until the project is complete, except holidays and severe weather.

What happens if you find a surprise in the walls?

We stop, show you, and engineer a fix. On a 1901 Wash Park home we found a foundation problem mid-project, stabilized it right away, and designed a permanent solution. Read that story.
