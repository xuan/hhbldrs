About H&H Builders

An engineer’s remodeling company, since 1998.

Two things set us apart. Our owner is a licensed structural engineer, and one in-house team handles your project from the first sketch to the final punch list.

John leads the design-build team on every project. His engineering background is why H&H can take on work other remodelers turn down, and why what we build is structurally sound.

  • 40+ years in residential construction and remodeling
  • Master of Science in Structural Engineering
