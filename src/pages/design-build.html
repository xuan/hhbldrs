The homeowner’s guide to design-build in Denver.

What design-build is, how it compares to hiring an architect and then bidding the job out, and what each step costs and how long it takes. Written by the team that builds what it designs.

The problem design-build solves

Here’s the usual story. A homeowner hires an architect, pays 8–15% of the budget for plans, and sends them out to contractors. The bids come back at two or three times what they planned to spend. Now they either borrow more, cut the design, or shelve plans they’ve already paid for. One industry estimate says only about 30% of residential remodeling designs ever get built.

The cause is simple: the people drawing the plans aren’t the people pricing and building them. Design-build puts one team, and one contract, behind all of it.

Who should consider design-build?

  • You want to move walls, add space or change the structure, not just refresh finishes
  • You have a firm budget and want to design to it
  • You’d rather have one accountable team than manage an architect and a contractor yourself

If you already have finished architectural plans and just need them built, a traditional bid may suit you. Call us anyway and we’ll tell you honestly.

10 questions to ask before hiring a design-builder

  1. Who does the structural engineering, and are they on staff?
  2. When do we set the budget: before or after design?
  3. What does Phase 1 cost, and what do I own if I stop there?
  4. Who is my single point of contact during construction?
  5. How do you handle surprises found when walls are opened?
  6. Can I see three projects like mine, with their budgets?
  7. Who pulls permits and handles historic review if needed?
  8. What’s your warranty, and what does it cover?
  9. Can we live at home during construction?
  10. Can I talk to past clients? (Ours are on GuildQuality.)
